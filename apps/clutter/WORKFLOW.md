# Clutter operating workflow

1. Seller submits item, photos/video, asking price and location.
2. Agent requests missing media and verifies condition/authenticity.
3. Agent records proposed buy price and expected resale price.
4. Manager approves, adjusts or rejects the proposed purchase.
5. Approved purchase becomes company inventory and records purchase cost.
6. Agent publishes the verified item for resale.
7. Buyer contacts the company through WhatsApp or the eventual checkout flow.
8. Sale records realized revenue; dashboard calculates gross margin and item-level profitability.

## Roles
- Seller: submits and follows an item.
- Agent: verifies, values, purchases and lists inventory.
- Manager: approves purchase price and controls margins.
- Admin: manages users, settings and reporting.
- Buyer: browses verified stock and initiates purchase.

## Profit model
Gross margin = resale price - approved buy price - recorded item expenses.
