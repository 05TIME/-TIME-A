# The African Record TV — Channel Profile

- Channel: https://youtube.com/@theafricanrecordtv
- Handle: @theafricanrecordtv
- Primary timezone: Africa/Lagos
- Editorial language: English by default; add subtitles or local-language editions when audience data supports them.
- Positioning: African stories, people, power, ideas and innovation — researched, contextualized and made engaging.
- Content mode: mixed subjects under one recognizable editorial promise, not unrelated uploads.

## Editorial pillars
1. African affairs, civic accountability and public-interest investigations.
2. African history, culture, identity and untold stories.
3. Business, economy, entrepreneurship and innovation.
4. Technology, science and future-facing ideas.
5. People, interviews, podcasts and explainers.

Every story should fit at least one pillar and make the audience promise clear in the first 10 seconds. Investigations must distinguish verified facts, allegations, analysis and unanswered questions. Use primary documents where possible, seek response from named subjects, and correct errors transparently.

## Initial schedule policy
Do not hard-code a daily publishing quota. Start with a sustainable experiment: 3 Shorts and 1 long-form video per week. Review after 14 days and adjust using channel impressions, click-through rate, average view duration, percentage viewed, returning viewers, subscribers gained and production capacity. The schedule optimizer must not scale output on tiny samples or trade accuracy for volume.

## Audience development
- Use Shorts to test hooks and introduce viewers to a deeper long-form story.
- Link related videos with playlists, end screens and pinned context where appropriate.
- Ask specific, non-manipulative questions that invite informed discussion.
- Use community posts for polls, follow-up questions and story leads.
- Repackage a strong story only when the new cut adds context or a distinct viewer benefit.

## Brand and editorial guardrails
- Be evidence-led, fair and transparent.
- No fabricated claims, fake engagement, misleading thumbnails, copied scripts or unlicensed footage/music.
- Clearly label opinion, sponsored content and synthetic/altered media when applicable.
- Do not publish allegations as established facts.
- Preserve source notes, release/rights information and corrections with each content package.
